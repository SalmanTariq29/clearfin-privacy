Privacy Policy

HisaabPro

Simple, private & offline expense tracking — Made in Pakistan 🇵🇰

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

🔒

Your privacy is our top priority. HisaabPro is built as a fully offline, local-only application. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device — always.

01

Information We Collect

HisaabPro does not collect any personal information. The app operates entirely offline. All data you enter — expenses, categories, budgets — is stored locally on your device only.

  • No account or registration required
  • No names, emails, or phone numbers collected
  • No financial data leaves your device
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting tools
02

Local Data Storage

All app data is stored locally using your device's internal storage. This includes:

  • Expense entries and categories you create
  • Budget limits and preferences you set
  • App settings and theme preferences
Important: If you uninstall HisaabPro, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted. We recommend using the export feature regularly to back up your data.
03

Internet Access & Permissions

HisaabPro does not require an internet connection to function. The app may request the following permissions on Android:

  • Storage — to export your data as a file (CSV/PDF)
  • Internet — only used if you tap "Rate on Play Store" or "Contact Us" which opens external links in your browser
No data is sent to any server at any time. Internet access is only triggered by your explicit action.
04

Third-Party Services

HisaabPro does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect data, including:

  • No Firebase or analytics platforms
  • No advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • No crash reporting services (e.g. Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • No social media integrations

The only third-party dependency used is the url_launcher package, which simply opens URLs in your device browser — no data is exchanged.

05

Children's Privacy

HisaabPro is a general-purpose personal finance app suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal data whatsoever, the app inherently complies with children's privacy requirements including COPPA. No parental consent is needed as no information is collected from any user.

06

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). We recommend:

  • Enabling device screen lock and encryption
  • Keeping your Android OS up to date
  • Exporting and backing up your data regularly
07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. Since HisaabPro collects no personal data, changes are unlikely to affect you in any meaningful way. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions or Concerns?

We're happy to answer any privacy-related questions. Reach out to us directly.
